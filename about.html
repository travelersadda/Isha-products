Our Story

Built to Solve Bangalore's Water Challenges

Isha Products was founded with a simple goal — make clean, soft, dependable water accessible to every Bangalore household and business. From hard borewell water to unreliable summer supply, we saw the everyday struggles families and facility managers faced, and built a company around solving them properly: right-sized systems, honest advice and fast support.

Today, we design, supply, install and service water softeners, solar water heaters, heat pumps, filtration systems, commercial RO plants and pressure pumps — backed by a team that shows up when you need us.
